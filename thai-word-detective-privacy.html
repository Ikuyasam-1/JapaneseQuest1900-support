Privacy policy

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app Thai Word Detective 1000, published by Inoue Masayuki / MASA BOOKS.

Data in the app

The app stores scores, answers, review schedules, and session progress locally on your device. It does not transmit these records to the publisher or a server. It includes no user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking. It does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photo-library access.

Offline recordings

All learning data and audio are bundled in the installed app. Playback takes place on the device. Using the recordings does not send audio or learning progress to Microsoft or the publisher.

Retention and deletion

Local learning records remain until reset or app removal. Reset local progress in Settings to clear them. Device backups may contain local app data under your Apple backup settings. The app does not operate its own cloud synchronization service.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes the App Store purchase under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not receive your payment-card details and contains no in-app purchase or subscription system. Apple may separately process store or system diagnostics according to your choices and its policies.

Support messages

If you email support, the publisher receives the email address and information you choose to send, to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Send only information needed for support. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to any records that must be retained.

This support website

These static support pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts, cookies set by this page, accounts, or forms. The hosting provider processes ordinary connection information needed to serve the pages. Clicking an email link opens your chosen email application.

Contact and changes
