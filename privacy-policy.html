Privacy policy

Japanese Quest 1900 · Inoue Masayuki
Updated November 1, 2026.

This policy distinguishes the currently released iOS version 1.0.0, which has no advertising or in-app purchases, from iOS version 1.1.0, currently distributed for TestFlight testing. Version 1.1.0 introduces home-screen banner advertising and optional one-time advertising removal through Apple's in-app purchases. Its public release is still pending.

Learning progress

Your answers, XP, stages, review list and preferences are stored on your device. The game does not send these learning records to the publisher, Google or a leaderboard. All 1,900 questions can be studied offline.

You can export a progress backup and choose where to save or share it. A backup contains learning records and preferences. Importing a backup replaces local learning progress. Reset removes local learning progress. Uninstalling the app can also remove it.

Audience

The app is intended for English-speaking Japanese learners aged 13 and over. It does not create learner accounts. Version 1.1.0 does not use personalized advertising or request Apple's tracking permission.

Advertising in iOS version 1.1.0

The free version can display Google AdMob banner advertising on the home screen. Ads require an internet connection. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers when available, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information to deliver, measure and protect advertising.

We configure ads with personalization disabled, a general-audience content rating, and the publisher first-party ID disabled. We do not request Apple's tracking permission or add a separate analytics SDK. These settings do not mean the advertising SDK collects no data.

Where required, Google's consent message appears before ads can be requested. Available consent settings can be reopened through Settings → Ads, purchases & privacy → Privacy choices. If consent or purchase status cannot be established, learning remains available.

Customers with verified Remove Ads access or a verified purchase of the earlier paid version do not receive ads. The app does not initialize the advertising SDK for those customers.

Google's processing is described in the Google privacy policy. Report ad opens an editable email containing the app version and, when available, a reference identifying the displayed ad. You choose whether to send it.

One-time purchases

Remove Ads is a non-consumable purchase processed by Apple. It is not a subscription. The localized price is shown before confirmation. Apple handles payment details; the publisher does not receive your card or bank information.

The app uses Apple-signed purchase information on your device to enable and restore ad-free access and recognize previous paid-app purchasers. Purchases are separate from progress backups. Use Settings → Ads, purchases & privacy → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for purchase. Apple may require a sign-in. A refund or revoked entitlement can remove access to the Remove Ads purchase.

Support and changes

Contact ikuyasam58@gmail.com about privacy or ad reports. We receive the information you choose to include in an email and use it to respond to your request. We may update this policy when app behavior changes.
