A little help for your next discovery.

Support for Thai Word Detective 1000, the Thai vocabulary game for English-speaking learners on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Email ikuyasam58@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and the question ID if a word needs correction. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

How do I play?

Open a case to answer 50 clues. Choose a Thai word or English meaning, or tap word parts in order and check the result. Read the explanation and use Listen or Slow. Correct answers earn points and streak bonuses.

Where are my missed words?

Open Review. Missed words are ready immediately. Correct answers return after 1, 3, 7, and 21 days of practice.

Can I play offline?

The iPhone/iPad app includes all 1,000 questions and 16 chapter audio files. No account or additional audio download is needed after installing the app.

I cannot hear the audio.

Raise the device's media volume and check the audio output, including Bluetooth headphones. Tap Listen or Slow again. If a recording fails, contact support with the question ID.

How is progress stored?

Scores, review schedules, and the open case are saved locally. Settings → Reset local progress clears these records after confirmation. Removing the app may remove progress. Device backups may include local data according to your Apple settings.

What is included in the purchase?

A single App Store purchase includes the full collection and bundled audio. There are no subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases. The App Store shows the price for your country or region. Apple handles purchases and refunds.

About the learning material

Based on Thai Vocabulary 1000: Learn by Breaking Words Down. Audio uses Microsoft Azure synthetic Thai speech at normal and slow speeds. Learner pronunciation is a teaching notation, not full IPA. Word-building images help memory and do not establish historical word origins.
